Privacy Policy

Life in the UK: Test Prep · last updated 9 September 2026

Life in the UK: Test Prep (“the app”) is an independent study aid for the UK citizenship test. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the UK Home Office, UK Visas and Immigration, or any government body. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

What the app stores on your device

Your study progress — answered questions, scores, streak, bookmarked questions, notes you have marked as read, your chosen language, and your optional name and test date — is saved only in your browser or device storage (localStorage). It stays on that device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it.

Accounts

You can use the entire app without an account by choosing “Continue without an account”.

If you do create an account, we collect the email address and password you provide, solely to create and secure that account and to let you sign back in. This is handled by Firebase Authentication, a service operated by Google LLC, acting as our data processor. Your password is stored only in hashed form by Firebase and is never visible to us. On the website you may instead sign in with Google, in which case Google shares your name and email address with the app for the same purpose.

We use your email address only for authentication and, if you request it, to send a password-reset email. We do not use it for marketing and we do not sell or share it.

What we do NOT do

  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No third-party trackers or social-media pixels.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data.
  • No collection of location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: open the account menu and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your Firebase Authentication record and the progress saved for that account on the device. Progress saved as a guest can be cleared from the app’s settings (“clear data”) or by removing the app.

Data location and retention

Account data held by Firebase Authentication is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure, which may be located outside the UK, including in the United States. It is retained until you delete your account.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
