Privacy Policy

Life in the UK: Test Prep · last updated 13 September 2026

Life in the UK: Test Prep (“the app”) is an independent study aid for the UK citizenship test. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the UK Home Office, UK Visas and Immigration, or any government body. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

What the app stores on your device

Your study progress — answered questions, scores, streak, bookmarked questions, notes you have marked as read, your chosen language, and your optional name and test date — is saved only in your browser or device storage (localStorage). It stays on that device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it.

Accounts

You can use the entire app without an account by choosing “Continue without an account”.

If you do create an account, we collect the email address and password you provide, solely to create and secure that account and to let you sign back in. This is handled by Firebase Authentication, a service operated by Google LLC, acting as our data processor. Your password is stored only in hashed form by Firebase and is never visible to us. On the website you may instead sign in with Google, in which case Google shares your name and email address with the app for the same purpose.

We use your email address only for authentication and, if you request it, to send a password-reset email. We do not use it for marketing and we do not sell or share it.

Your free trial

In the iPhone app, a new account gets 24 hours of full access with no ads. To know when that started, we store one date and time per account in Google Cloud Firestore, linked to your account identifier. That is the only thing that record contains — no study data, no scores, no email address. It is written once, when you first sign in, and is never changed afterwards.

There is no trial and no such record on the website, which is free to use in full.

Advertising (iPhone app only)

After the trial, the app stays free and is paid for by advertising, unless you buy the one-off unlock described below. Ads are supplied by Google AdMob (Google LLC). The website carries no advertising at all.

To serve ads, AdMob may use your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA), approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads. Google acts as an independent controller for this data; how Google uses it is described in the Google Business Data Responsibility and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choice. Before any ad is requested, the app shows you a Google consent form (UMP) so you can accept or refuse personalised advertising, and iOS separately asks you for App Tracking Transparency permission. If you refuse either, the app works exactly the same — you simply see non-personalised ads. You can change your mind at any time in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

We do not see, receive, or store your advertising identifier ourselves.

Purchases

A single one-off in-app purchase permanently unlocks the quiz and removes all advertising. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your card details, billing address, or Apple ID. The app only asks Apple whether this device’s Apple ID owns the unlock; that check happens on the device.

What we do NOT do

  • No analytics and no usage tracking by us.
  • No advertising at all on the website, or in the app once you have bought the unlock.
  • No sale of personal data.
  • Your study progress, scores and answers are never uploaded — they stay on your device.
  • No collection of location, contacts or photos.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: open the account menu and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your Firebase Authentication record, the trial date-and-time record described above, and the progress saved for that account on the device. Progress saved as a guest can be cleared from the app’s settings (“clear data”) or by removing the app.

Deleting your account does not cancel a purchase: the unlock belongs to your Apple ID, not to your account here, and can be restored at any time with Restore purchase in Settings.

Data location and retention

Account data held by Firebase Authentication, and the single trial record held in Cloud Firestore, are stored on Google Cloud infrastructure, which may be located outside the UK, including in the United States. Both are retained until you delete your account. Data handled by Google AdMob is retained by Google under its own policies, linked above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
